This section covers tools developed by the Parrot team, and third-party tools whose integration in Parrot is worth documenting: where they are preinstalled, how to reach them from the menu, or how they are packaged in our repositories.

It is deliberately not a collection of general tutorials. Parrot ships thousands of packages, and their upstream documentation is more complete and better maintained than anything we could mirror here. For general usage, refer to the project's own manual or `man <tool>`.
